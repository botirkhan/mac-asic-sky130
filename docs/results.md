## Same RTL on the Artix-7 FPGA (for comparison)

From [pipelined-mac-accelerator](https://github.com/botirkhan/pipelined-mac-accelerator) (xc7a100tcsg324-1, Vivado 2026.1, out of context): timing met at 250 MHz with WNS +0.874 ns (about 320 MHz), and the whole datapath packs into **1 DSP48E1** plus 8 flip-flops. On SKY130 there is no hard DSP block, so the 16×16 multiplier and 40-bit adder are built from standard cells, which is where the area above goes.
